About

Applied econometrics for energy and finance policy

My work sits where energy economics, environmental economics and finance meet. I ask practical questions: who bears energy-price shocks, how risk travels between energy and financial markets, and which financial instruments actually move investment toward clean energy.

Before research, I spent eight years in retail and trade banking in Karachi, including foreign-trade management at Summit Bank. That background shapes how I model pricing and financing decisions as institutions and households actually make them. I have led a policy-oriented research grant from proposal to dissemination as Principal Investigator for the CAREC Institute, and I now teach economics and finance at Bahçeşehir Cyprus University.

Research

Three research streams

Stream 1

Energy markets and economic resilience

Energy-price volatility, market interconnectedness and shocks, and how they pass into exchange rates, equity and debt markets, and the wider economy.

  • Energy price volatility and exchange-rate instability
  • European energy markets after COVID-19
  • Geopolitical risk and China's energy markets
Stream 2

Energy transition and sustainable finance

Green bonds, clean-energy investment, fintech and digital currencies, and financial globalisation as channels that help or hold back sustainable investment.

  • Green bonds and clean-energy investment
  • Fintech, digital currencies and renewable stocks
  • Energy poverty and affordability
Stream 3

Climate policy and sustainable development

How institutional quality, policy design and market-based instruments shape environmental outcomes, including emission trading in Central Asia.

  • Emission trading in CAREC economies
  • Governance, economic freedom and SDG 3
  • Circular economy and resource efficiency
Emerging agenda

Digital transformation and AI

I am extending this work to how AI, digitalisation and financial technology affect productivity, resource efficiency, investment and environmental outcomes, including the electricity demands of AI infrastructure.
